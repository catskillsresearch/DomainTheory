# References

The first four linked sections in [arxiv.md](arxiv.md) are working transcriptions (LLM vision OCR) of Dana Scott’s original documents. The fifth section summarizes cross-presentation bridge theorems from the separate Lean package cited below.

## Original documents (transcriptions)

1. **Continuous lattices (1972)** — corresponding section: [1972_ContinuousLattices.md](1972_ContinuousLattices.md).  
   Scott, D. Continuous lattices. In *Toposes, Algebraic Geometry and Logic*, edited by F. Lawvere. Springer-Verlag, Lecture Notes in Mathematics, vol. 274, 1972, pp. 97–136.

2. **Data types as lattices (1976)** — corresponding section: [1976_DataTypes.md](1976_DataTypes.md).  
   Scott, D. Data types as lattices. *SIAM Journal on Computing*, vol. 5, 1976, pp. 522–587. (Oxford Programming Research Group Technical Monograph PRG-5, September 1976.)

3. **Lectures on a mathematical theory of computation (PRG-19, 1981)** — corresponding section: [1980_Neighborhood.md](1980_Neighborhood.md).  
   Scott, D. S. *Lectures on a Mathematical Theory of Computation*. Technical Monograph PRG-19, May 1981. Oxford University Computing Laboratory, Programming Research Group. © 1981 Dana S. Scott / University of Oxford.

4. **Domains for denotational semantics (1982)** — corresponding section: [1982_InformationSystems.md](1982_InformationSystems.md).  
   Scott, D. Domains for denotational semantics. In *Automata, Languages and Programming* (ICALP 1982), Lecture Notes in Computer Science, vol. 140, Springer, 1982, pp. 577–613.
