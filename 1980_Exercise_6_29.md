# Exercise 6.29

**EXERCISE 6.29.** Generalize $+$ and $\times$ to infinitary operations on domains:

$$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} D_n \quad \text{and} \quad \prod_{n=0}^{\infty} D_n.$$

Would a similar generalization be possible for $\oplus$ and $\otimes$?
