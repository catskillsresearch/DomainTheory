# functionSpaceProjection

`functionSpaceProjection` is the projection of \([D \to D]\) in \([D' \to D']\) induced by a projection of \(D\) in \(D'\). `functionSpaceProjection.retrIncl` says the retraction after the inclusion is the identity, and `functionSpaceProjection.inclRetrLe` says the inclusion after the retraction lies below the identity. The two maps are [functionSpaceInclFun](1972_functionSpaceInclFun.md) and [functionSpaceRetrFun](1972_functionSpaceRetrFun.md).
