# embInfInf

`embInfInf` is the embedding of the tower limit into its function space, $i_\infty(x) = \bigsqcup_n i_{n\infty} \circ x_{n+1} \circ j_{\infty n}$. `embInfInf.apply` is that formula and `embInfInf.preservesDirectedSup` is preservation of directed suprema. The comparison with the projection back is [projInfInf](1972_projInfInf.md) and [embInfInf_projInfInf](1972_embInfInf_projInfInf.md).
