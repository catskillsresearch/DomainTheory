# functionSpaceInclFun

`functionSpaceInclFun` is the inclusion of function spaces induced by a projection, \(f \mapsto i \circ f \circ j\). `functionSpaceInclFun.continuous` is continuity of the underlying map, and `functionSpaceInclFun.outer_continuous` is continuity of the assignment \(f \mapsto i \circ f \circ j\).
