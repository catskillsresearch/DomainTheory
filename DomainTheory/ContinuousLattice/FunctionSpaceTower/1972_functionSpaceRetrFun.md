# functionSpaceRetrFun

`functionSpaceRetrFun` is the retraction of function spaces induced by a projection, \(g \mapsto j \circ g \circ i\). `functionSpaceRetrFun.continuous` is continuity of the underlying map, and `functionSpaceRetrFun.outer_continuous` is continuity of the assignment \(g \mapsto j \circ g \circ i\).
