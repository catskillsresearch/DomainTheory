# jInfTerm

`jInfTerm` is the n-th summand of the projection back onto the limit, \(f \mapsto i_{(n+1)\infty}(j_{\infty n} \circ f \circ i_{n\infty})\). `jInfTerm.apply` is that formula. The summands of the embedding are [iInfTerm](1972_iInfTerm.md).
