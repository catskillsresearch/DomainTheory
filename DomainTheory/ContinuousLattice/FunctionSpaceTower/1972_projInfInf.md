# projInfInf

`projInfInf` is the projection from the function space back onto the tower limit, \(j_\infty(f) = \bigsqcup_n i_{(n+1)\infty}(j_{\infty n} \circ f \circ i_{n\infty})\). `projInfInf.apply` is that formula, `projInfInf.preservesDirectedSup` is preservation of directed suprema, and `projInfInf.comp_embInfInf` says \(j_\infty \circ i_\infty = \mathrm{id}\). The other composite is [embInfInf](1972_embInfInf_projInfInf.md).
