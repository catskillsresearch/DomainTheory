# Continuous lattice applications

Definition 2.3 says a complete lattice is continuous when every element is the supremum of the elements way below it. Proposition 2.4 is the same condition written with open neighbourhoods. Theorem 2.12 is the agreement of an injective space with a continuous lattice.
