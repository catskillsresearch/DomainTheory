# Proposition 3.2

**3.2 Proposition.** *The induced partial ordering on $[X \to Y]$ is such that:*

$$f \sqsubseteq g \quad \text{iff} \quad f(x) \sqsubseteq g(x) \text{ for all } x \in X,$$

where $f, g \in [X \to Y]$. $\square$

where $f, g \in [X \to Y]$. $\square$
