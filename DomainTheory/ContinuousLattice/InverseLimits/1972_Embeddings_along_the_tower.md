# Embeddings along the tower

The partial inverses of the bonding projections $j_n$ are the embeddings $i_n$. `embLE` climbs from an earlier stage to a later one by composing those embeddings. The lemmas are `embLE_self`, `embLE_succ`, `embLE_succ_left`, `embLE_le`, `embLE_mono`, and `embLE_preservesDirectedSup`.
