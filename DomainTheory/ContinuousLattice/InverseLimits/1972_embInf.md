# embInf

`embInf` is [embInfFun](1972_embInfFun.md) as a Scott map. `embInf_succ` is the recursion \(i_{n\infty} = i_{(n+1)\infty} \circ i_n\). `embInf_le_succ` and `embInf_family_directed` say that the embeddings of the coordinates of a thread form a monotone, hence directed, family.
