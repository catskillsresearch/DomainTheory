# Coordinates of an embedding

`iComp` is one coordinate of the embedding $i_{n\infty}$ of a stage into the limit: it climbs by `embLE` at later stages and descends by `projLE` at earlier ones. The lemmas are `iComp_of_le`, `iComp_of_ge`, `iComp_self`, `iComp_incl_le`, `iComp_preservesDirectedSup`, `iComp_monotone`, and `iComp_compatible`.
