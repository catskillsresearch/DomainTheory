# Directed suprema

[`inverseLimit.eq_iSup`](1972_inverseLimit.md) says each thread is the directed supremum of its embeddings from the stages, $x = \bigsqcup_n i_{n\infty}(x_n)$. `idInf_eq_iSup` says the identity on the limit is the supremum of the approximating projections $i_{n\infty} \circ j_{\infty n}$.
