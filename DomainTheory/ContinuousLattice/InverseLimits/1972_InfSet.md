# InfSet

`instInfSet` is the infimum of a set of threads. `retr_sInf` and `compatible_sInf` show that the pointwise infimum is a thread, `coe_sInf` is the underlying sequence of that infimum, and `isGLB_sInf'` says it is a greatest lower bound.
