# projLE

`projLE` is the composite of the bonding projections from a later stage down to an earlier one. The lemmas are `projLE.self`, `projLE.succ`, `projLE.mono`, `projLE.compatible`, `projLE.retr`, and `projLE.preservesDirectedSup`.
