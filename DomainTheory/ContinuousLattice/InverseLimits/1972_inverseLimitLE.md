# inverseLimitLE

`inverseLimitLE` is the coordinatewise order on threads: every coordinate of the first lies below the corresponding coordinate of the second. `inverseLimitLE.refl`, `inverseLimitLE.trans`, and `inverseLimitLE.antisymm` are the proofs the partial order uses.
