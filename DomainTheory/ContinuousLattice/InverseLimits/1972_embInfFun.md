# embInfFun

`embInfFun` is the thread \(i_{n\infty}(x)\). `embInfFun_coe` is its underlying sequence, `embInf_monotone` is monotonicity, and `embInf_preservesDirectedSup` is preservation of directed suprema. The Scott map is [embInf](1972_embInf.md).
