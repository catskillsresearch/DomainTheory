# The limit lattice

The limit becomes a complete lattice in the steps below. `inverseLimitPartialOrder` is the coordinatewise order. `inverseLimitSInfCoe` is the coordinatewise infimum of a set of threads. `instInfSet` packages that infimum once it is shown to be a thread, and records that it is a greatest lower bound. `instCompleteLattice` obtains the complete lattice from that order and those infima. `coe_sSup_of_directed` is the pointwise formula for a nonempty directed supremum.
