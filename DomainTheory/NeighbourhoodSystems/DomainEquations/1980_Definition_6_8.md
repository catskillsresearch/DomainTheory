# Definition 6.8

**DEFINITION 6.8.** On the category of domains and strict approximable maps a functor $T$ is *continuous on maps* if for any systems $D$ and $E$ the induced mapping

$$
\lambda f.\, T(f) : (D \to_\bot E) \to (T(D) \to_\bot T(E))
$$

is approximable.
