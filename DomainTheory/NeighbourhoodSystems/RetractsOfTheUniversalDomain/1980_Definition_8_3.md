# Definition 8.3

## Human-English

**DEFINITION 8.3.** A retraction $a : E \to E$ is called a *projection* provided

$$a \sqsubseteq 1_E;$$

it is *finitary* iff its fixed-point set is isomorphic to a domain. $\square$
