# Are two neighbourhoods nested or disjoint?

It should be noted that, in these three examples, the reason that we have a neighbourhood system is a simple consequence of a very special circumstance: in these systems two neighbourhoods are either disjoint or one is included in the other. This arrangement of neighbourhoods is by no means necessary.
