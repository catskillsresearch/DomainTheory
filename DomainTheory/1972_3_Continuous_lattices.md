# 3. Continuous lattices

Specialization opens the section. Scott-open sets are next, then Proposition 2.1, the way-below relation, and Proposition 2.2. Definition 2.3 is the continuous lattice. Proposition 2.4, the continuous maps, and Milner's correction follow. Proposition 2.5 is the criterion the corrected proof of Proposition 2.10 uses, so that proof comes next. The paper's own proof is kept at its place in the numbering, and it fails. Propositions 2.6 through 2.11 and Theorem 2.12 follow.
